# Quantum Analytics E-commerce Retail Data Analysis Projects

A collection of data analytics projects done with Quantum Analytics focused on e-commerce, retail, customer behaviour, sales, marketing, and hospitality. This repository showcases Excel and Power BI analysis, dashboards, business insights, and data-driven findings from real-world datasets.

---

## 📁 Projects in This Repository

### 🛍️ [Amazon E-Commerce Sales Power BI Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Amazon%20E-Commerce%20Sales%20Power%20BI%20Project)
Built a star-schema Power BI model from a single Amazon product dataset to explore how price, ratings, reviews, and discounts connect — and whether premium products really earn their higher price tag.

### 📈 [Digital Marketing Excel Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Digital%20Marketing%20Excel%20Project)
A Q1 Digital Marketing & ROI Performance Dashboard tracking ad spend, revenue, and ROMI across campaigns, dates, and geographies, to find out which campaigns actually pay off and which ones lose money.

### 🏨 [Hotel Bookings](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Hotel%20Bookings)
An analysis of 117,399 hotel bookings from two hotels in Portugal (2015–2017), uncovering cancellation patterns, seasonal revenue trends, and which customer segments are the biggest cancellation risk.

### 🛒 [Retail Sales & Customer Demographic Excel Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Retail%20Sales%20%26%20Customer%20Demographic%20Excel%20Project)
An Excel dashboard turning 1,000 raw retail transactions into insights on customer spending, product categories, gender, and age groups, using combo charts and interactive slicers.

### 🏬 [Tata Online Retail Power BI Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Tata%20Online%20Retail%20Power%20BI%20Project)
A consultant-style revenue and customer analysis built for a fictional online retail store's leadership team, featuring a full RFM customer segmentation model (High Value, Regular, One-Time) and strategic growth recommendations.

### 👗 [Women Clothing E-commerce Power BI Project](https://github.com/DataWithMowa/E-commerce-Retail-Data-Analysis-Projects-/tree/main/Women%20Clothing%20E-commerce%20Power%20BI%20Project)
An analysis of 23,486 customer reviews, examining how ratings and recommendation rates differ across product categories and customer age groups — and spotlighting the one category that consistently underperforms.
